# TAWAL Marketing & Customer Engagement Plan

A student-level marketing portfolio project about how TAWAL could strengthen brand awareness, digital engagement, and communication with relevant B2B stakeholders.

> **Project status:** Independent university portfolio proposal. It is not commissioned, endorsed, or reviewed by TAWAL. The campaign, personas, objectives, and recommendations are student-created ideas.

## Project outline

The report covers TAWAL’s public business description, a basic market overview, a student-level SWOT, stakeholder groups, two fictional personas, three proposed SMART objectives, a simple marketing strategy, the “Behind the Network” campaign, a one-month content calendar, marketing KPIs, practical recommendations, and a conclusion.

The proposed calendar is for **2–30 November 2026**. Dates are planning suggestions and can be changed.

## Research and limitations

Research was checked on **5 October 2026**. TAWAL, PIF, CST, IHS Towers, and Zain public sources were used, with official sources prioritized. The current TAWAL company page reports the combined portfolio and ownership structure. PIF’s annual report provides historical confirmation of the acquisition and consolidation. A 2018–2019 IHS/Zain transaction announcement is used only as a historical comparable business-model example, not as evidence of current competitor rankings.

No internal data, customer list, revenue, market share, or campaign performance has been invented. Personas are fictional composites. The SWOT and all recommendations are the student’s analysis. Performance targets are proposed pilot goals, not TAWAL-approved commitments or forecasts.

**All figures in the KPI dashboard are hypothetical sample data.** They are included only to demonstrate the workbook layout and calculations. Replace them with authorized analytics before using the dashboard for a real campaign.

## Suggested review before use

If this plan were adapted for an actual company campaign, the service descriptions, visuals, links, customer examples, technical statements, sustainability claims, and campaign targets should be reviewed by the relevant company teams. Targets should be adjusted using a real baseline.
