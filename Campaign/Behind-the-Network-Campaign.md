# Behind the Network

**Campaign status:** Student concept mockups — not official or published TAWAL content.  
**Proposed pilot:** Four LinkedIn posts across November 2026.

## Campaign idea

I developed “Behind the Network” to explain, in everyday language, some of the physical infrastructure behind connectivity. Each post covers one small idea: what infrastructure does, how colocation works, why a building may need an indoor solution, and what information could help start an infrastructure conversation.

The campaign is not intended to suggest that TAWAL provides mobile subscriptions or to promise a particular network outcome. The descriptions below are drafts based on TAWAL’s public service information and should be checked by the company’s technical and communications teams before use. [1] [2]

## Marketing objective

For a four-week pilot, publish one post per week and record impressions, reactions, comments, shares and link clicks for all four posts. The broader goal is to make TAWAL’s infrastructure role easier for relevant B2B stakeholders to understand. A proposed performance target in the report is a 10% increase in average impressions compared with the four weeks before the pilot, if a comparable baseline is available. This is a planning target, not a forecast or a TAWAL-approved commitment.

## Target audience

The main audience is network and connectivity teams that may explore telecom infrastructure. A second audience is property owners, venue managers and large-project stakeholders who may need to understand indoor coverage or site requirements. These groups are proposed for the student plan; they are not a confirmed company segmentation.

## Key message

> The services people use every day depend on physical digital infrastructure. TAWAL provides infrastructure and related services that organizations can use to support connectivity.

## Content pillars

1. **The physical side of connectivity:** explain the infrastructure provider’s role in simple terms.
2. **Infrastructure sharing:** introduce colocation without unverified savings or coverage claims.
3. **Indoor coverage:** explain that building coverage may need a different discussion from outdoor sites.
4. **Starting a conversation:** show the basic information that can help a relevant business inquiry.

## Channel and call to action

Use LinkedIn for one post per week. Link to an existing official TAWAL solution or contact page. Suggested calls to action are **“Explore TAWAL solutions,” “Learn about colocation,” “Explore In-Building Solutions,”** and **“Contact TAWAL.”** Use tagged links if analytics access is available.

## KPIs

- **Impressions:** how many times each post was shown, using LinkedIn’s available definition.
- **Engagement:** reactions, comments and shares.
- **Engagement rate:** in the accompanying dashboard, engagements divided by impressions.
- **Link clicks:** clicks on the approved solution or contact link.
- **Follower growth:** difference between the page follower count at the start and end of the pilot.
- **Inquiries:** relevant contact or service requests only if they can be linked to the pilot and counted using the company’s normal process.

The dashboard’s numbers are sample data only and are not TAWAL results.
