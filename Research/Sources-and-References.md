# Sources and references

Research checked on **5 October 2026**. Official company and regulator sources are prioritized. Historical announcements are identified as such; they are not treated as current performance data.

1. **TAWAL — About Us.** Company history, ownership after the TAWAL/GLIC combination, reported portfolio footprint, business model, and public service description. [https://www.tawal.com.sa/en/about-us](https://www.tawal.com.sa/en/about-us)
2. **TAWAL — Solutions.** Public descriptions of built-to-suit, colocation, in-building solutions, fiber facility, smart pole, and flexible tower offerings. [https://www.tawal.com.sa/en/solutions](https://www.tawal.com.sa/en/solutions)
3. **Public Investment Fund (PIF) — “PIF and stc Group sign definitive agreements to form region’s largest telecom tower company,” 22 April 2024.** Announcement of the planned 51% acquisition and TAWAL/GLIC combination; used as a historical announcement, not as proof of completion. [https://www.pif.gov.sa/en/news-and-insights/press-releases/2024/pif-and-stc-group-sign-definitive-agreements-to-form-regions-largest-telecom-tower-company/](https://www.pif.gov.sa/en/news-and-insights/press-releases/2024/pif-and-stc-group-sign-definitive-agreements-to-form-regions-largest-telecom-tower-company/)
4. **PIF — Annual Report 2024.** Confirms PIF’s acquisition of 51% of TAWAL from stc and its consolidation with Golden Lattice Investment Company. [https://www.pif.gov.sa/-/media/project/pif-corporate/pif-corporate-site/our-financials/annual-reports/pdf/20250904_pif_ar24_public_english_interactive-pdf.pdf](https://www.pif.gov.sa/-/media/project/pif-corporate/pif-corporate-site/our-financials/annual-reports/pdf/20250904_pif_ar24_public_english_interactive-pdf.pdf)
5. **Communications, Space & Technology Commission (CST) — “CST Issued the Saudi Internet Report 2024,” published 12 May 2025.** Public digital-use context for Saudi Arabia; not a tower market-size estimate. [https://www.cst.gov.sa/en/media-center/news/CST-Issued-the-Saudi-Internet-Report-2024](https://www.cst.gov.sa/en/media-center/news/CST-Issued-the-Saudi-Internet-Report-2024)
6. **IHS Towers — “IHS Continues Middle East Expansion Strategy Through Signed Sale and Lease Back Agreement with Zain in Saudi Arabia,” 27 March 2019.** Historical announcement describing a comparable passive-infrastructure sale-and-leaseback arrangement. [https://www.ihstowers.com/support-and-info/media/press-releases/2019/ihs-continues-middle-east-expansion-strategy-through-signed-sale-and-lease-back-agreement-zain-saudi-arabia](https://www.ihstowers.com/support-and-info/media/press-releases/2019/ihs-continues-middle-east-expansion-strategy-through-signed-sale-and-lease-back-agreement-zain-saudi-arabia)
7. **Zain Group — “Zain Saudi Arabia agrees to sell and lease back its telecom tower network to IHS Holdings for circa USD 648 million,” 28 November 2018.** Historical announcement corroborating the planned Zain KSA/IHS arrangement. [https://zain.com/en/press/ZainSaudiArabia_IHS](https://zain.com/en/press/ZainSaudiArabia_IHS)
